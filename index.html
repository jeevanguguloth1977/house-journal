How a project runs

From concept to complete execution

  1. Consultation

    We visit or review your space and listen to how you live and what you want to spend.

  2. Design

    Layouts, materials and lighting are drawn up so you can see the plan before work starts.

  3. Budget and materials

    Finishes and fittings are chosen to fit the budget you set.

  4. Execution

    Our team builds and installs the interiors and hands over a finished space.
